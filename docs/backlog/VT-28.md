**30-second summary:** in the 2026-09-03 benchmark the vteam arm declared 38 criteria done, each
with an e2e test as evidence; the judge's production-build probes fail three of them (weight 7).

spec differently. Find out, with a reproduction, and fix the gate or the doctrine — not the arm.

failed — and had claimed"). Until the cause is known, "proof, not promises" is a claim the
benchmark contradicts. Spec: docs/benchmark/2026-09-03/SCORECARD.md §"Did the claims hold?",
arm-a at `356bfcc` (owner's machine). no UI.
